name: frontend-visual-design
description: Design an accessible, credible, NASA-inspired TrendEarth AI climate data dashboard, charts, diagrams, and visual assets after requirements approval.
---

1. Use `docs/04-DESIGN-BRIEF.md` and the verified data contract.
2. Prioritize a legible chart, axes/units, provenance, selectors and interpretation before hero visuals.
3. Support keyboard, small mobile viewport and light/no motion; don't rely on color alone for significance.
4. Never display fabricated scientific measurements without a prominent mock placeholder label; final build uses real NASA data only.
5. Don't imply NASA endorsement or reuse logos/imagery without reviewing applicable rights.
6. Submit wireframe/visual review before introducing large frontend dependencies or animations.
