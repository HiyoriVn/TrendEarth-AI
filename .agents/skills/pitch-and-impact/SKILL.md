3. Write one clear five-slide narrative and 5-minute script; prioritize relevance and validity.
4. Distinguish educational potential, implemented impact, future AI features and scientific measurements.
5. Include NASA attribution, data grid/OLS limitations and disclosure of tools/AI assistance per organizer rules.
6. Never claim a trained LSTM, improved accuracy or deployed forecast without demonstrable evidence.
