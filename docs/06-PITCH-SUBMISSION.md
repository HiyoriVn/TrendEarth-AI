## One-sentence pitch
*TrendEarth makes NASA Earth-system time series easier to investigate by converting gridded observations into transparent, interpretable evidence of environmental trends.*

## Scientific FAQ
**Is this AI?** The project name is TrendEarth AI; MVP uses transparent statistical trend analysis. AI-assisted variable discovery is a possible future extension, **not** a deployed claim.
**Can you prove local urban heat islands?** No; two gridded T2M locations cannot isolate that causal mechanism.
**Does p < 0.05 prove climate change?** No; it is conditional statistical evidence for a fitted slope under model assumptions.
